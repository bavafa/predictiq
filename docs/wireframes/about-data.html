What this is

PredictIQ is a prototype asset-intelligence application built on a public predictive-maintenance sample dataset. It predicts recorded component failures over a 24-hour horizon and links model outputs to transparent, assumption-based maintenance prioritization. It is a demonstration of industrial analytics and product design, not a deployed production system.

How to read the labels

Historical

Simulated sample data: sensor readings, errors, maintenance and failures as recorded in the sample. Not real plant, fleet or mine data.

Predicted

Model estimate: the chance of a recorded failure in the next 24 hours, and what pushed it up or down.

Illustrative

Assumption you can change: costs, crew slots and how much maintenance lowers risk. Not real costs.

"Historical replay" steps through old records in order. Nothing on this site is live.

Data source

Data: Microsoft Predictive Maintenance Modelling Guide sample dataset (simulated), from github.com/microsoft/sqlworkshops (pinned commit e836f4e). Microsoft states content in that repository is licensed under CC BY 4.0; the repository's license file is MIT.

  • 100 machines, hourly sensor readings, 2015-01-01 06:00 to 2016-01-01 06:00.
  • Also: error logs, maintenance records, failure records, machine model and age.
  • Sensor units are not stated by the source.
  • The replay covers — to —, the period the model never saw while training.

Known limitations

  • Simulated data; results may look better than on real machines.
  • Tested on the same machines at a later time only.
  • Explanations are patterns, not causes.
  • Costs are illustrative. No savings are claimed.

Not part of this demo

  • Live sensor feeds or control of any equipment.
  • Real maintenance costs or work orders.
  • Accounts, personal data, chatbots.
