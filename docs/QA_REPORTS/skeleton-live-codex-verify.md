# Trial deploy live checks: independent review

Reviewer: Codex (gpt-6-sol, medium effort), read-only, 14 Nov 2026. It reviewed the committed evidence and did not contact the preview. The lead's response follows the review.

## Findings

- **High — The six-check hosting gate has not passed as written.** Check 2 requires the deployed Python function **with the real model bundle** to be measured under 500 MB uncompressed. The supplied `vercel inspect --json` evidence gives 6,479,971 bytes for the skeleton function, with compression unspecified; the reported 11,081,986-byte local uncompressed figure is an estimate with no raw record. Check 4 requires the largest allowed response **for each endpoint**. Only the skeleton’s health and metadata endpoints exist. [Decision 53](/workspace/predictiq/DECISIONS.md:125), the “all met” entry in [TASKS.md](/workspace/predictiq/TASKS.md:41), and [STATUS.md](/workspace/predictiq/STATUS.md:58) overclaim the gate.
- **Medium — Check 5 is only partly demonstrated.** The [cache log](/workspace/predictiq/docs/QA_REPORTS/skeleton-live-evidence/cache.log) confirms three metadata HITs, two new-query MISSes, one repeated-query HIT, and two uncached health responses. It does not contain the claimed initial metadata MISS or show the full shared-cache header. The [rate-limit log](/workspace/predictiq/docs/QA_REPORTS/skeleton-live-evidence/ratelimit.log) records 52 × 429 and 68 × 200 only at the temporary **20/60 s** limit. At **300/60 s**, 330 authenticated requests produced 330 × 200. The final 300/60 s active configuration is supported by the supplied settings evidence, but its 429 behavior was not observed.
- **Medium — Cold-start wording is stronger than the measurement.** The ten idle-copy requests have median **1.4983 s**, best **1.3318 s**, worst **1.6607 s**; ten immediate repeats have median **0.3897 s**. These are client-side total request times, including network, and “cold” is inferred from idle copies of the same build rather than confirmed by function telemetry. The roughly **1.11 s** median gap is suggestive, not a measured runtime-only cold-start cost. Fresh redeploy first requests, correctly excluded, ranged **0.4197–0.6806 s**.
- **Low — Some reported numbers lack a raw trace or sample label.** The 1.55 s supporting sample, its roughly 35-minute idle period, the claimed initial cache MISS, the local uncompressed byte count, and “at least five” egress addresses cannot be checked in the supplied logs. The “combined 14” median of **1.47 s** is arithmetically correct **if** the unlogged 1.55 s sample is included; the logs contain only 13 of those 14 requests. The report gives no sample count for its endpoint size measurements. Its ten screenshots exist, and the sampled desktop and mobile images show rendered pages, but screenshots alone do not establish all ten HTTP 200 statuses.

The remaining reported counts and sizes reconcile: 36-byte health and 422-byte metadata responses appear in the logs; the 10,879-byte home response appears in the rate-limit log; 52 + 68 = 120; and the supplied deployment inspection supports 6,479,971 bytes and Python 3.12. A read-only scan of all **164 tracked files** found no candidate committed secret value, including an automation-bypass token or private key. The supplied project settings also support that deployment protection remained on and that one automation bypass entry existed.

## Verdict: **FAIL**

The skeleton preview demonstrates working hosting, but checks **2 and 4 are unmet** against the explicit [architecture criteria](/workspace/predictiq/docs/architecture.md:133). Check **5 is partial**. Decision 53’s unqualified “all six met” conclusion is therefore not justified; it should remain conditional until the real bundle and full endpoint responses are measured and the cache and limit evidence gaps are resolved.
